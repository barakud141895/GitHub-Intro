## My favourite math formulas

Euler’s formula: connects the exponential function with trigonometric functions, where $e$ is Euler’s number, $i$ is the imaginary unit and $\varphi$ is an angle in radians.

$$
e^{i\varphi} = \cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi
$$

Pythagorean theorem: in a right triangle, $a$ and $b$ are the legs and $c$ is the hypotenuse.

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

Quadratic formula: gives the roots $x_{1,2}$ of the equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are its coefficients.

$$
x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

Gaussian integral: the area under the curve $e^{-\alpha x^2}$, where $\alpha$ is a positive constant and $\pi$ is the circle constant.

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\alpha x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{\alpha}}
$$

Law of sines: connects the sides $a$, $b$ and $c$ of a triangle with the opposite angles $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$, where $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle.

$$
\begin{cases}
\frac{a}{\sin\alpha} = 2R \\
\frac{b}{\sin\beta} = 2R \\
\frac{c}{\sin\gamma} = 2R
\end{cases}
$$

Wave formula: connects the speed of light $c$ with the wavelength $\lambda$ and the frequency $\nu$.

$$
c = \lambda\nu
$$

Hooke’s law: connects the stress $\sigma$ with the strain $\varepsilon$, where $E$ is Young’s modulus.

$$
\sigma = E\varepsilon
$$

Identity matrix: the matrix $I_n$ of size $n \times n$ with ones on the main diagonal and zeros elsewhere.

$$
I_n = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & \cdots & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
